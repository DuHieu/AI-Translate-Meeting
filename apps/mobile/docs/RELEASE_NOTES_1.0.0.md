# Laya Meet Mobile 1.0.0 — Beta release notes

**What to test**
- Create or join a meeting from your phone
- Check camera / mic before joining
- Confirm video of other participants appears
- Confirm bilingual captions update while people speak
- Toggle mute, camera, flip, speaker
- Background the app and confirm audio continues (Android notification / iOS lock screen)
- Leave the meeting and confirm you return to the home screen

**Supported**
- Android 8+ (API 26+) recommended
- iOS 14+

**Please report**
- Crashes, frozen video, missing captions, permission issues, and battery drain during long calls

Thank you for helping validate the first mobile beta of Laya Meet.
